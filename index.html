Personal app

kiosko

A private Mac app that turns the newsletters I subscribe to into one calm page, starting with my local cinema.

What it does. kiosko reads a few specific newsletters and ticket confirmations from one Gmail account of mine, pulls out what matters (films, sessions, talks, tickets) and shows them on a single screen. Newsletters it has fully understood can be moved to Gmail's Bin; everything else stays where it is.

Who it is for. Only me. kiosko is not distributed, has no sign-up and no other users.

Where data lives. On my Mac. There is no kiosko server.
