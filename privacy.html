Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026.

kiosko is a personal macOS app used only by its author. It has no server, no accounts of its own, no analytics and no advertising.

Google user data kiosko accesses

With the owner's permission (OAuth scope gmail.modify), kiosko reads messages from a short list of known senders in one Gmail account: cinema newsletters and ticket confirmations. It never lists or reads mail outside those senders.

How the data is used

  • Messages are parsed on the Mac to extract films, sessions, talks and bookings, which are shown in the app.
  • Newsletters that were fully understood may be moved to Gmail's Bin, and only after the owner turns that on. Ticket confirmations are never deleted or archived.
  • Gmail data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train any AI or machine-learning model.

Storage

Extracted information is stored locally on the Mac. The Google sign-in token is stored in the macOS Keychain. Nothing is uploaded to a server run by kiosko.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared with anyone. To show film details, kiosko looks up film titles on TMDB and public Letterboxd pages; those requests contain only a film title or id, never email content or email addresses.

Limited Use

kiosko's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Deleting the app's data folder on the Mac removes everything kiosko stored.

Contact

Questions: open an issue in the kiosko-site repository.
